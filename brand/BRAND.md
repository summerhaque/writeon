# Write On! — Brand

*Everyone has a story worth telling, and every voice deserves the chance to be heard.*

Bright, encouraging, confident. A real studio for young writers: light paper-blue, lots of white space, round friendly shapes, and one warm amber for rewards and progress. Grown-up enough that a teacher takes it seriously, warm enough that a nine-year-old wants to open it.

## The mark

Three written lines, with a pencil finishing the middle one: sky-blue lines, a navy graphite tip, an amber eraser. It comes in two cuts:

- **Standard:** longer lines and thinner bars. Use it in logos, and anywhere the mark is 48px or larger.
- **Icon cut:** shorter lines and thicker bars. Use it for anything small: the favicon, app icons, tab bars, and any mark under 48px.

The wordmark is Plus Jakarta Sans ExtraBold and lives in the lockup files. Never retype it by hand.

## Clear space and minimum size

- **Clear space:** keep at least the height of one written line clear on every side.
- **Full lockup:** never narrower than 120px.
- **Mark:** never smaller than 20px. Below 48px, always use the icon cut.
- Don't recolor, stretch, rotate, outline or add effects.

Contrast checks: navy on the page is 12.0:1, muted text 5.7:1, white on primary 5.3:1, navy on amber 6.3:1.

**Genres:** Fantasy `#6B4FD0`, Sci-Fi `#0E7E99`, Mystery `#4A5BA8`, Comedy `#B4651A`, Friendship `#C2453F`. Each also has a soft tint for backgrounds.

**The nine stages** use one blue ramp, from `#C9E5FB` (Idea) to `#1F6FB2` (Publishing). Done stages fill solid, the current stage gets a 3px amber ring, and later stages are outlines. Never grey a stage out so hard that it looks locked: any stage can be opened.

## Type

**Plus Jakarta Sans**, one family, no serif (Google Fonts).

- **Headings:** ExtraBold (800) with slightly tight tracking (−0.02em).
- **Body:** Regular, 16–17px, line height about 1.6.
- **Kids' reading copy:** never below 15px.

## The motif

The three written lines from the mark appear as small accents: a section eyebrow, a loading state, or a divider.
